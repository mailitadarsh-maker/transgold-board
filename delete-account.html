Delete Your Account & Data

Developer: Pips-Tech (Pips Technology LLP)

You can request deletion of your Feelani account and all associated personal data at any time. Feelani is developed and published by Pips-Tech (legal entity: Pips Technology LLP), Ernakulam, Kochi, Kerala, India. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

1
Send an email to support@transgoldmarkets.com from the email address registered on your account.
2
Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the name and phone number on your account so we can verify it's you.
3
We will confirm your request by reply email and process the deletion within a reasonable timeframe.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted: your name, email address, phone number, and password (hash)
  • Deleted: your login and authentication tokens

Once deletion is complete, your account cannot be recovered and you would need to sign up again to use the app in the future.

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of confirmation. You'll receive a final confirmation email once your data has been removed from our systems.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process, contact us at support@transgoldmarkets.com.

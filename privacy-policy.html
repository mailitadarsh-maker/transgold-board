Privacy Policy — Feelani

Last updated: July 15, 2026
Developer: Pips-Tech (Pips Technology LLP)

This Privacy Policy explains how Feelani ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Feelani mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play and the App Store. Feelani is developed and published by Pips-Tech (legal entity: Pips Technology LLP), Ernakulam, Kochi, Kerala, India. We built this App to keep data collection minimal — here's exactly what we do and don't collect.

✓We collect your name, email, and phone number to create and manage your account.
✕We do not collect or access your device's location.
✕We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting tools.
✕We do not send push notifications. All alerts appear inside the App only.
✕We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name — to personalize your account
  • Email address — used for account verification, password reset, and account-related communication
  • Phone number — used for account identification and support
  • Password — never stored in plain text. Your password is hashed using industry-standard hashing before being saved, and we cannot see or recover your original password

We do not collect any other personal information beyond what is necessary to create and secure your account.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location data: The App does not request or access your device's GPS or location information in any form.
  • Analytics or crash data: The App does not integrate any analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services (such as Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Sentry).
  • Push notification tokens: The App does not use push notifications. All price alerts and updates are delivered within the App while it is open.
  • Advertising identifiers: The App does not display ads and does not collect advertising IDs.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Create, verify, and maintain your account
  • Authenticate you when you log in
  • Allow you to reset your password if you forget it
  • Communicate important account-related information, such as email verification and password-reset links
  • Provide customer support if you contact us

4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored or transmitted in plain text. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

The App also includes device-integrity checks that block access on rooted, jailbroken, or tampered devices, as an added layer of protection for your account and data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your information is not shared with any external analytics, advertising, or data-broker services, because we do not use any.

We may disclose information only if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Feelani, Transgold Markets, our users, or the public.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. If you wish to have your account and associated data deleted, you may contact us using the details below, and we will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
